ZeroMargin: stop the timer at exactly 1.000 s

Tap to start, tap to stop, and see how close to zero margin you can get. A perfect-timing stopwatch game where every millisecond counts, with daily targets, bots and 2-player duels.

What ZeroMargin does

Stop the clock

Pick a target of 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 seconds, or any custom time from 0.5 to 60 s. Tap anywhere to stop as close to it as you can, with your personal best for each target.

Fair, lag-free timing

Both taps use the touchscreen's own timestamps, so screen lag cancels out. Your error is shown in milliseconds.

Daily target

A new target every day, like 2.347 seconds, the same for every player. Three attempts, best one counts.

Rank tiers

Climb from Rookie through Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond to Zero. Unlock achievements like Laser (within 10 ms).

Bots and 2-player duels

Take on Easy, Medium or Hard bots, or pass the phone for a best-of series against a friend.

Neon arcade style

A dark arcade look with glowing neon accents, particle bursts and crisp haptics. Vibration can be switched off.

Questions

How precise is the timer?

Results are measured in milliseconds. A perfect stop is 0 ms off the target.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Duel mode is a pass-the-phone best-of series on a single device.

Is there an online leaderboard?

No. The daily target is the same for everyone, and you can share your result, but scores are kept on your phone.

Can I try it in the browser?

Yes, play the online stop-the-timer game. The app adds daily targets, ranks, bots and duels.

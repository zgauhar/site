Dates people count down to

  • Expiry and renewal dates: passport, ID card, driver's licence, visa, insurance, warranties.
  • Best-before and use-by dates on food and medicine.
  • Holidays and events: Christmas, New Year, birthdays, weddings, exams, due dates.
  • Deadlines: tax returns, contract notice periods, subscription renewals.

Questions

How are the days counted?

The result is the number of calendar days from the start date to the target date, not counting the start day. Tomorrow is 1 day away.

What are working days?

Monday to Friday between the two dates, counting the target date but not the start date. Public holidays aren't removed because they differ by country.

Can I get reminded before a date?

Yes. The Days Until app counts down to expiry and renewal dates (food, medicine, passports, licences) and reminds you 30 days, 1 week and 1 day before.

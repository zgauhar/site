Smart Quiz: a daily trivia challenge

Answer a fresh general-knowledge quiz every day, keep your streak alive, level up and collect achievements. Build custom quizzes from the categories you love, and keep playing offline.

What Smart Quiz does

A daily trivia quiz

A quick multiple-choice quiz every day. Each completed quiz grows your streak, and an evening reminder makes sure you don't lose it.

Levels, XP and achievements

Correct answers earn XP; daily quizzes, long streaks and perfect rounds earn bonus XP. Unlock achievements like Flawless, On Fire and Centurion.

Share your grid

Share your score as a 🟩🟥 grid with your streak and challenge friends to beat it.

Custom quizzes

Pick categories (science, history, geography, art, sports, film, music, video games, nature, mythology and more) and 5 to 50 questions per round.

Offline challenge

A bank of questions is saved on your phone, so you can play on flights, the subway or anywhere without signal.

Quiz-night practice

A 2-minute daily brain workout for trivia fans, students and pub-quiz teams.

Questions

Where do the questions come from?

From the Open Trivia Database, used under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Smart Quiz keeps a bank of questions on your phone for offline play.

How do streaks work?

Finish the daily quiz each day to grow your streak. With Plus you get two streak freezes a month for days you miss.

Which categories are there?

Science, history, geography, art, sports, film, music, video games, nature, mythology and general knowledge, among others.

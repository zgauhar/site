Trivia Showtime: the trivia game show with a talking host

Trivia as a TV game show, with you as the contestant. Your host reads every question out loud and reacts to every answer. Ten rounds, one category pick per round, and seven right answers win the show. Formerly Smart Quiz.

What Trivia Showtime does

How the show works

Ten rounds, each harder than the last. Pick one of four categories every round, use your one pass, get seven right to win, then go for a perfect ten. A fourth wrong answer and you're out.

A host who talks back

Your host reads every question out loud and reacts to every answer: clapping, cheering, facepalming and occasionally fainting. Skip a line, or switch the voice off and read along.

Meet the cast

Eight hosts, from showman Max Marquee and superfan Penny Sparks to Granny Gert, Captain Quizbeard, UNIT-7 and Duchess Pemberton. Dress them up with hats, glasses and more.

Thousands of questions

14 categories, from film and TV to science, history, geography and sports. Every question has a difficulty level from 1 to 10, and questions you've seen don't repeat.

Play your way

No energy, lives or coins. A 20-second timer, a relaxed 40 seconds or no timer at all, and a short host mode when you just want the questions. Works offline.

Track your run

Daily streak, wins, best score, perfect shows and accuracy. Share your score and challenge your friends.

Questions

Where do the questions come from?

From the Open Trivia Database, used under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, with a difficulty level from 1 to 10 for every question.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. The whole question bank is on your phone, so the show works offline.

Where does the host's voice come from?

From your device's text-to-speech engine, so voices vary a little between phones. You can pick a different voice for each host, or switch the voice off.

Is this the old Smart Quiz?

Yes. Trivia Showtime replaces Smart Quiz on Google Play, so if you had Smart Quiz installed, it updates to Trivia Showtime.

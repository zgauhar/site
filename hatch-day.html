Countdown for every clutch

Day counter and countdown to hatch day, with the candling days, lockdown and hatch window on one clear timeline.

Today's targets

Temperature and humidity for today, in °C or °F, for forced-air and still-air incubators. Cooling and misting days for ducks and geese.

Reminders that fit your day

Questions

Does it work with my incubator?

Yes. Hatch Day doesn't connect to the incubator; it works with any model, forced-air or still-air, automatic turner or hand turning.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Everything is stored on your phone and works offline. See the privacy policy.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish and Indonesian.

Where do the targets come from?

From widely published values in incubator makers' manuals and university poultry guides. Always follow your incubator's manual and check the temperature with a calibrated thermometer.

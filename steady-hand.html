Steady Hand: how steady is your hand?

Hold your phone as still as you can and the motion sensor scores every tiny movement. Get your rank, beat your record and challenge your friends.

What Steady Hand does

The hold-still challenge

Pick 5, 10 or 15 seconds, follow the 3-2-1 countdown and hold steady. A live bullseye and motion wave show every wobble.

Score and rank

Each attempt gets a steadiness score from 0 to 100 and a rank from S to D. Try different grips to push for an S.

Daily challenge

A new target every day, such as "score 75+ on a 10-second test". Beat it to grow your streak.

Achievements

Ten to collect, from "First steady" to "Surgeon's hands".

Calm-focus breathing

Warm up with the built-in breathing guide, then re-test and see if a calmer start helps your score.

Challenge friends

Share your result in one tap and find out who really has the steadiest hand.

Questions

How does it measure steadiness?

It reads your phone's motion sensor while you hold still and turns the movement into a score from 0 to 100.

Is it a medical test?

No. Steady Hand is for entertainment and personal curiosity only. It is not a medical device and does not measure, diagnose or treat any health condition.

Can several people play?

Yes. Share your score with friends, or use Pro party mode for 2 to 6 players on one phone.

Who is it for?

Photographers, gamers, artists, game nights and anyone who likes a quick focus challenge.

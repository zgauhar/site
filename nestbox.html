Nestbox: rabbit breeding app

A record book for rabbit breeders: breedings, kindlings, kits, weights and pedigrees, on your phone and offline. For show rabbitries, meat rabbits and homesteads.

iPhone version coming soon.

From the breeding to the keeper

The barn, card by card

A hutch card for every doe and buck with ear number, breed, variety, age and hutch. A stamp shows where each doe stands: bred, palpate, pregnant, nest box, due, late, nursing or open.

Breeding dates worked out

Log a breeding and get the palpation day, nest box day and kindling date, with reminders at the time you choose. Set your own timing for palpation, nest box and weaning.

Kindling and kits

Record kits born alive and dead. Every live kit gets its own record: sex, colour, weights, sold, culled or kept. A kept kit joins the barn with its parents and birthday.

Litter weights

Weigh the whole litter kit by kit, with the total and the day-21 milk weight. Weights in lb/oz or kg/g.

Pedigrees

Four generations filled in from your barn or typed from a paper pedigree, with ear numbers, varieties, weights and GC numbers. Printable PDF with your rabbitry name (Pro).

Records and show jumping

Litter size, kits weaned and survival per doe. Pro ranks your does, tracks sales and income, exports CSV and keeps show-jumping starts with personal bests.

Questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Everything is stored on your phone and works offline. You can save a backup file to Google Drive, e-mail or a computer and restore it on a new phone. See the privacy policy.

Where do the dates come from?

From common rabbit breeding practice: kindling about 31 days after the breeding (28 to 35 is normal), palpation at day 10 to 14, the nest box at day 27 to 29 and weaning at 4 to 8 weeks. You can change the days that differ in your barn. Nestbox is a record book, not veterinary advice.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Czech, Hungarian, Swedish and Danish.

Sire & Dam: dog breeding app

Heats, progesterone, matings, pregnancy, puppies and buyers: everything about your litters in one place, on your phone. For hobby and professional dog breeders.

Coming soon to Android and iPhone.

From the first day of heat to the puppy’s new home

Heats and next heat

Log the first day of each heat. Sire & Dam learns her average interval, predicts the next heat and reminds you a week before.

Progesterone and ovulation

Enter results in ng/mL or nmol/L and see the stage at a glance: baseline, LH surge, ovulation, fertile. Estimated ovulation and the best days to breed for natural, fresh, chilled and frozen semen.

Whelping countdown

Due date from ovulation (63 days, ±1) or from the mating (58–68 days), with reminders for the ultrasound, more food, X-ray, whelping box and temperature checks.

Temperature log

Twice-daily readings with an alert when her temperature drops before whelping, plus the signs that mean it’s time to call the vet.

Every puppy, every gram

Collar colours, weigh the whole litter in one go, daily gain in %, and an alert when a puppy loses weight or hasn’t doubled its birth weight by day 10.

Buyers and puppy packs

Waitlist, deposits and reservations, and a PDF puppy pack for new owners with parents, health record and weights. (Pro)

Questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Everything is stored on your phone and works offline. You can save a backup file to Google Drive, iCloud Drive or e-mail and restore it on a new phone. See the privacy policy.

Where do the dates and values come from?

From standard veterinary reproduction references: ovulation at a progesterone of about 4–8 ng/mL, whelping 63 days after ovulation, and the WSAVA and ESCCAP puppy guidelines. Labs, breeds and dogs vary, so the app gives guidance: always follow your vet.

Which languages?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.

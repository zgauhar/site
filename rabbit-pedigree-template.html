How to fill in a rabbit pedigree

  1. Start with the rabbit on the left: name, ear number, variety and current weight.
  2. Sire on top, dam below in each pair, going right: parents, grandparents, great-grandparents.
  3. Copy from the parents' pedigrees. The sire's and dam's own pedigrees give you the generations behind them.
  4. Add registration and grand champion numbers where a rabbit has them.
  5. Sign and date it before it goes to the buyer.

Frequently asked questions

How many generations does a rabbit pedigree need?

Most clubs ask for at least three generations (parents, grandparents and great-grandparents) for registration. This template has room for all three plus the rabbit itself.

What goes on a rabbit pedigree?

For each rabbit: name, ear number (tattoo), variety or colour, weight, and any registration or grand champion numbers. The top has the rabbitry, breeder, date of birth and sex.

Is my pedigree saved?

What you type stays in this browser on this device only, so you can come back to it. Nothing is uploaded. Print it or save it as a PDF to keep a copy.

Can I print pedigrees from my own records?

Yes, with the Nestbox app: it fills the pedigree in from the parents in your barn and prints a PDF with your rabbitry name and a signature line (Pro).

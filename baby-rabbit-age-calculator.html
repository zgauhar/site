Why the nest can look empty

A doe nurses her kits only once or twice a day, for a few minutes, usually at night or at dawn. Seeing her stay away from the nest is normal. Kits that are fed have round bellies and lie quietly in a heap; kits that are not fed are thin, wrinkled and restless. If they look like that, ask your vet or an experienced breeder quickly.

When to wean rabbits

  • 4 weeks is the earliest weaning age; kits weaned this young need extra care.
  • 6 weeks is common in meat rabbitries.
  • 8 weeks is common for show and pet rabbits, and is the default here and in Nestbox.

Many breeders wean the biggest kits first and leave the smallest with the doe for a few more days.

Frequently asked questions

When do baby rabbits open their eyes?

At about 10 to 12 days old. The ears open around the same time.

When do baby rabbits leave the nest?

At about 3 weeks, when they start to hop out and nibble hay and pellets. The nest box usually comes out then, later in cold weather.

How often does a mother rabbit feed her babies?

Only once or twice a day, for a few minutes, usually at night or dawn. That is why she seems to ignore the nest.

What age can baby rabbits be weaned?

From 4 weeks at the earliest; 6 weeks is common for meat rabbits and 8 weeks for show and pet rabbits.

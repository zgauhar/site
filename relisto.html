From photo to listing in seconds

AI listing from a photo

Photo in, title, description, category, brand, size, condition and colour out, in one of 10 languages. Add a photo of the label for better details.

Check before you post

Details the AI is unsure about are marked amber so you fix them before buyers see them. Chest, length and waist can go straight into the text.

Copy for any marketplace

Copy the text for Vinted, eBay, Depop and more, with the title cut to fit each limit. You paste and publish yourself, so your accounts stay safe.

Your real profit

Sale price minus what you paid, fees and postage, per item, per month and per year. Split one purchase cost across several items, or import past sales from a CSV file.

DAC7 counter

Marketplaces report sellers after 30 sales or about €2,000 a year on one platform. Relisto counts each platform and projects when you will reach a limit. How DAC7 works.

Stale-stock hints

Items online for 30 days or more get a suggested price drop, and a checklist reminds you where else an item is listed when it sells.

Questions

What is Relisto?

Relisto is a resale listing helper for Android and iOS. You take a photo of an item and it writes the title, description, brand, size and condition in your language. It also logs your sales and profit and counts your sales per marketplace against the DAC7 reporting limits.

Does Relisto post to Vinted or eBay for me?

No. Relisto never logs in to a marketplace and never posts for you. You copy the text and publish it yourself, which keeps your accounts within every platform's rules.

Which marketplaces and languages does it support?

It is made for sellers on Vinted, eBay, Kleinanzeigen, Wallapop, Leboncoin, Subito, OLX, Depop, Marktplaats, Vestiaire Collective and Facebook Marketplace. Listings and the app are available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Czech and Lithuanian.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: 5 AI listings a month, unlimited manual items, the sales and profit log and the DAC7 counter. Pro adds 100 (Basic), 300 (Plus) or 500 (Max) AI listings a month, bulk mode, the value check, reports and CSV export, and no ads. Prices are shown in the app in your currency.

Where do my photos and sales go?

Your items and sales stay on your phone. Photos are sent to the Relisto server only when you ask for an AI listing, and they are not kept there. See the privacy policy.

Is the DAC7 counter tax advice?

No. It is a guide that counts the sales you mark as sold per platform. Platforms may count refunds and cancellations differently. Ask your tax office or a tax adviser if you are unsure.

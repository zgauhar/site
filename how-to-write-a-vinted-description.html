How to write a Vinted description (10 templates)

Short answer: a Vinted description that sells starts with the brand, item and size, states the condition honestly (with any flaw shown in a photo), gives measurements for clothes and shoes, and ends with one line about postage or bundles. Below is a title formula, a checklist and 10 templates you can copy and edit.

The title formula

Brand + item + key detail + size + colour. Use the words a buyer would type into search, and leave out filler such as “beautiful” or “must have”.

  • Zara black midi wrap dress M
  • Nike Air Force 1 white trainers UK 7
  • Levi’s 501 straight jeans W32 L32 blue

What to put in every description

  • First line: brand, item and size again, with the size system (UK 10 / EU 38).
  • Condition: new with tags, like new, very good, good or fair, and how often it was worn. Name any flaw and point to the photo.
  • Measurements: measured flat. Pit to pit and length for tops, waist and inside leg for trousers, insole length for shoes.
  • Material and fit: from the care label, and whether it runs small or large.
  • Practical details: postage speed, bundles, smoke-free or pet-free home.

What to avoid

  • Lists of brands or keywords the item is not (against marketplace rules and confusing for buyers).
  • Writing only in capital letters, or a description that only says “see photos”.
  • Hiding flaws. A flaw you mention rarely causes a problem; one the buyer finds can lead to a return.

Questions

What should a Vinted description include?

Brand, item type, size (and the size system), colour, material, condition with any flaws, and measurements for clothes. Buyers search by these words and decide on them.

How long should a Vinted description be?

Short and complete: 4 to 8 lines is enough for most items. Put the most important facts first, because the start of the description shows in the preview.

What makes a good Vinted title?

Brand + item + key detail + size + colour, in the words buyers type, for example “Levi’s 501 straight jeans W32 L32 blue”.

Can I use other brand names to get more views?

No. Only describe the item you are selling. Marketplace rules forbid misleading keywords such as brands the item is not.

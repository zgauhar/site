Daily Budget: know what you can spend today

Add your income and bills once. Daily Budget spreads what's left across your budget cycle, carries unspent money forward and tells you every morning how much you can spend today. No bank login, no ads, no cloud.

What Daily Budget does

Your daily allowance

What's left after bills is spread over your budget cycle. Unspent money carries forward, and you see what's left today, this week and this month.

Starts on payday

Pick the day your budget cycle starts, so the numbers match your salary instead of the calendar.

Fast expense tracking

Add an expense in a few taps, with categories, subcategories and notes. Recurring income and bills: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or custom.

Streaks that help you save

Every day you stay within your allowance grows your streak. A small nudge that makes saving feel like a game.

Widget and reminder

A home-screen widget and an optional daily reminder show today's number without opening the app.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no bank connection, no ads. Back up to a file and restore on a new phone, or import expenses from CSV.

Questions

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Daily Budget never asks for a bank login. You add income, bills and expenses yourself, and everything stays on your phone.

How is the daily budget calculated?

Income minus bills (and savings, if you set them aside) is spread over the days left in your budget cycle. Spend less today and tomorrow's allowance goes up; spend more and it goes down.

Can I track more than one currency?

Yes, with Pro: each budget has its own currency and start day, which suits travel or a second household.

Does it have ads?

No. Daily Budget has no ads.

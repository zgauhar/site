Daily Budget: know what you can spend today

Add your income and bills once. Daily Budget spreads what's left across your pay cycle, carries unspent money forward and tells you every morning how much you can spend today. No bank login, no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

What Daily Budget does

Your daily allowance

What's left after bills is spread over your pay cycle. Unspent money carries forward, and you see what's left today, this week and this cycle.

See how it's worked out

Tap today's number for a step-by-step breakdown: income, bills, the daily allowance and the carryover from earlier days.

Spread big costs

Spread a big shop or bill over 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, or mark it paid from savings, so one purchase doesn't wipe out today.

Follows your payday

Monthly, weekly, every 2 weeks or twice a month. Add one-off income like tips or refunds, and choose how overspending carries over.

Fast expense tracking

Categories, subcategories and notes, one-tap re-log of your usual expenses, and edit anything later. Upcoming bills are shown before they're due.

Private by design

No account, no bank connection, no ads. A free widget, daily reminder and streak, backups to a file and CSV import.

Questions

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. Daily Budget never asks for a bank login. You add income, bills and expenses yourself, and your budget data stays on your phone.

How is the daily budget calculated?

Income minus bills is spread over the days left in your pay cycle, plus whatever you didn't spend on earlier days. Tap today's number in the app to see every step.

What if I have one big expense?

Spread it over several days or the rest of the cycle, or mark it as paid from savings, so it doesn't take all of today's allowance.

Can I track more than one currency?

Yes, with Pro: each budget has its own currency and pay cycle, which suits travel or a second household.

Does it have ads?

No. Daily Budget has no ads.

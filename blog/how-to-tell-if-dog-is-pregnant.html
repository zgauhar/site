How to tell if your dog is pregnant: ultrasound, relaxin test, palpation and X-ray timing

The reliable way to tell if your dog is pregnant is a vet check: a relaxin blood test or an ultrasound scan from about day 25 to 30 after ovulation, and an X-ray from about day 45 to count the puppies. Early physical signs are easy to miss, and a false pregnancy can look exactly like the real thing, so timing the tests well saves you guessing for weeks.

Dog pregnancy tests and when to do them

A dog pregnancy lasts about 63 days from ovulation, or 58 to 68 days from mating, because sperm can wait in the female for several days. Counting from ovulation, which a progesterone test can pinpoint, makes every date below more accurate.

MethodWhen (days after ovulation, approx.)What it tells you
Relaxin blood testFrom about day 25 to 30Pregnant or not. Too early or a very small litter can give a false negative.
Ultrasound scanFrom about day 25 to 30Pregnancy, puppy heartbeats and how they are developing. Not reliable for counting.
Abdominal palpation by a vetAround day 28 to 35Small swellings in the uterus. Harder to feel later, and should be done by a vet.
X-rayFrom about day 45, best in the last 1 to 2 weeksHow many puppies, and their size relative to the pelvis.

Your vet will tell you which test suits your dog and your timing. Many breeders book an ultrasound around four weeks after mating and an X-ray in the final week or two.

Signs of pregnancy you can see at home

  • Weeks 3 to 4: nipples may turn pinker and slightly larger. Some dogs are quieter, eat a little less or have a small, clear vaginal discharge.
  • Weeks 5 to 6: weight gain and a firmer, rounder belly; appetite usually increases.
  • Weeks 7 to 8: clear belly growth, enlarged mammary glands, and you may see or feel puppies moving.
  • Week 9: nesting, restlessness and a drop in body temperature 12 to 24 hours before labour. Our guide to whelping signs and the temperature drop covers this stage.

None of these signs proves a pregnancy on its own. Weight gain can be food, and nesting or milk can be a false pregnancy.

Pregnancy or false pregnancy?

A false (phantom) pregnancy is common in unspayed bitches about 6 to 9 weeks after a heat, whether she was mated or not. She may nest, guard toys, gain a little weight and even produce milk. Hormonally it looks like pregnancy, so only a scan or a relaxin test settles it. Mark the heat dates: our guide to dog heat cycle stages explains the timing.

Why the number of puppies matters

An X-ray count tells you when whelping is finished. If she stops straining and fewer puppies have arrived than the X-ray showed, call your vet. Once they arrive, weigh every puppy daily; the guide to newborn puppy weight gain shows what normal looks like.

Frequently asked questions

How soon can you tell if a dog is pregnant?

A vet can usually confirm it from about day 25 to 30 after ovulation, with an ultrasound scan or a relaxin blood test. Physical signs such as weight gain and a firmer belly usually appear only after about five weeks.

Can I use a human pregnancy test on my dog?

No. Human tests detect hCG, a hormone dogs do not produce. Dog pregnancy blood tests look for relaxin, a hormone made by the placenta, and are done at the vet or with a veterinary test kit.

When can an X-ray show how many puppies there are?

Puppy skeletons show up on X-ray from about day 45 after ovulation, and counting is most reliable in the last week or two before whelping. Knowing the number helps you tell when whelping is finished.

Could my dog have a false pregnancy?

Yes. A false (phantom) pregnancy can cause nesting, a swollen belly and even milk about 6 to 9 weeks after a heat, without any puppies. An ultrasound or relaxin test tells the two apart.

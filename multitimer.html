MultiTimer: multiple timers at once

Cooking a full dinner, timing workout sets or studying in focus blocks: every timer counts down on its own, with its own name, color and alarm. Countdown timers, stopwatches and Pomodoro timers run side by side.

What MultiTimer does

Many timers at once

Start, pause and reset each timer independently. Name and color-code every timer, and add or remove time on the fly.

Pomodoro focus timer

Set your work and break lengths and MultiTimer switches between them automatically.

Timer cards

Group timers into cards such as Kitchen, Workout or Study, and start or pause a whole card at once.

Alarms that don't miss

Exact alarms fire on time, even in battery saver and after a restart. Full-screen alarm over the lock screen, 10 sounds, looping and vibration.

Live in your notifications

A live countdown in the notification shade with Pause and Reset buttons.

Presets and history

Save favorite durations as presets and see every completed timer in the history log.

Questions

Can I run several timers at the same time?

Yes. That's what MultiTimer is for: as many countdown timers, stopwatches and Pomodoro timers as you need, each with its own alarm.

Will the alarm ring if my phone is asleep?

Yes. MultiTimer uses exact alarms that fire in Doze and battery-saver mode and after a restart, and can show a full-screen alarm over the lock screen when you allow it.

Does MultiTimer have ads?

No. There are no ads and no account. Your timers stay on your device.

Can I use it as a HIIT or Tabata timer?

Yes, with Pro: build interval sequences of work and rest steps and repeat them for any number of rounds.

How long are rabbits pregnant?

A rabbit's gestation is about 31 days. Most does kindle on day 31 or 32 after the breeding, and anything from day 28 to day 35 can be normal. Larger litters often come a little earlier and small litters a little later.

Example: a doe bred on 1 March 2027 can be palpated around 13 March, gets her nest box on 29 March and is due on 1 April 2027. Kits can arrive any day from 29 March to 5 April.

Signs a doe is about to kindle

  • Nest building: she carries hay into the box, often a few days before kindling.
  • Fur pulling: she pulls fur from her chest and belly to line the nest, often hours before the kits come. Some does only pull fur after kindling, especially first-time does.
  • Appetite: many does eat less on the last day.

False pregnancy in rabbits

A doe can ovulate without conceiving, after an infertile breeding or even after being mounted by another doe. She then acts pregnant for about 16 to 18 days and may pull fur and build a nest around that time. Palpating at day 10 to 14 tells you which it is, and a doe in a false pregnancy can usually be rebred once it ends.

Frequently asked questions

How many days is a rabbit pregnant?

About 31 days. Most does kindle on day 31 or 32 after the breeding, and day 28 to 35 can be normal.

When should I put the nest box in?

Around day 27 to 29 after the breeding. Earlier and many does use it as a toilet; later and she may kindle before it is in.

When can I palpate a rabbit for pregnancy?

From about day 10 to 14 after the breeding. The kits feel like small marbles in a row. Learn it from an experienced breeder or your vet, and press gently.

My doe is on day 33 and has no kits. What should I do?

Keep the nest box in and check her twice a day. Some does kindle on day 33 or 34. If there are still no kits by day 35, or she strains, bleeds or stops eating, call your vet.

- How to tell if a rabbit is pregnant: palpation from day 10 to 14, and the signs after: Rabbit pregnancy is easy to miss by eye until the last week. Palpating the doe 10 to 14 days after the breeding tells you early, so an empty doe can be rebred straight away.

- My rabbit just had babies: what to do in the first 24 hours after kindling: A doe that kindled overnight usually needs little help, but a quick nest check the same day catches cold kits, dead kits and a nest without fur. Here is what to look at and what is normal.

- How to tell if your dog is pregnant: ultrasound, relaxin test, palpation and X-ray timing: Early signs in a pregnant dog are easy to miss and easy to fake. A vet can confirm the pregnancy from about four weeks, and an X-ray late in pregnancy counts the puppies.

- Incubator power outage: how long can eggs survive without heat, and what to do: A power cut is not automatically the end of a hatch. Embryos slow down when they cool and often pick up again. Here is what to do during the outage and after the power comes back.

- How to budget on an irregular income: a 6-step plan for freelance, gig and tip earners: When pay changes every month, budget on the lowest month you can count on, pay yourself a steady amount from a holding account and give every extra euro a job before it arrives.

- What size cable for an induction hob or electric cooker?: A full-power induction hob is a 32 A load. Clipped to a wall on a short run that means 4 mm² copper; in an insulated wall 6 mm², and longer runs need 10 mm².

- How long should a power nap be? Set your nap timer right: Ten to twenty minutes refreshes you without the groggy wake-up. Set the timer a few minutes longer to allow for falling asleep, and nap before mid-afternoon.

- Endometriosis flare-ups: how to track triggers and what helped: Flares feel random until you write them down. Log the start, the end, the worst pain, the cycle day and what helped, and after two or three cycles a pattern often shows.

- How much weight should a newborn puppy gain per day?: Healthy newborn puppies gain roughly 5 to 10% of their body weight every day and double their birth weight within 7 to 10 days. Weigh them daily and the scale warns you before anything else does.

- What size cable for an electric shower? 7.5 to 10.5 kW: Electric showers are the biggest single-phase load in most homes. An 8.5 kW shower clipped to a wall needs 6 mm² copper; a 9.5 or 10.5 kW shower needs 10 mm², and 16 mm² in an insulated wall.

- Sinking funds: how to budget for Christmas, car repairs and other irregular expenses: Christmas, car insurance and annual subscriptions are not surprises, they just do not arrive monthly. A sinking fund turns each of them into a small monthly bill.

- Dog whelping signs: the temperature drop and what happens next: A bitch's temperature usually drops from about 38.5 °C to below 37.8 °C (100 °F) 8 to 24 hours before she whelps. Take it twice a day from day 58 after ovulation and watch for nesting.

- How to prepare for an endometriosis doctor appointment: Appointments are short and symptoms are hard to remember on demand. A one-page summary and five questions make the time count.

- How long can you store hatching eggs before incubating?: Set hatching eggs within 7 days of laying. Keep them pointed end down at 13 to 16 °C (55 to 60 °F), turn them once a day after the first few days and let them warm up before they go in.

- Timeboxing: how to use a countdown timer to finish tasks: Instead of working until a task is done, decide how long it deserves and stop when the timer rings. Here is how to set the box.

- How long do opened foods last in the fridge? A shelf-life chart: The date on the label is for an unopened pack. Once you open it the clock changes. Here is a chart of how long common foods keep in the fridge after opening.

- What size cable for a 7.4 kW home EV charger?: A 7.4 kW home charger draws 32 A for hours. For a short run clipped to a wall that means 4 mm² copper; in an insulated wall or over 30 m it means 10 mm² or more.

- HIIT and Tabata timer: how to set the work and rest intervals: Tabata is 20 seconds on, 10 off, eight times: four minutes in all. Other HIIT formats use longer work blocks and equal or longer rest.

- How often do dogs go into heat? Heat cycle stages, length and signs: Most dogs come into heat about every six months, and the visible heat lasts around three weeks. Knowing the four stages tells you when she is fertile and when the next heat is due.

- When to stop turning eggs: lockdown day for chicken, duck and quail: Stop turning eggs three days before they are due: day 18 for chickens, day 25 for ducks, day 14 for Coturnix quail. Raise the humidity, keep the lid shut and wait.

- Passport, ID and licence expiry: when to renew and how to never miss it: An expired passport is not found out at home, it is found out at the airport. Here is when to renew each document and how to get a reminder long before it matters.

- Pomodoro technique: how to set up your timer and adjust the 25/5 rhythm: Twenty-five minutes on, five off, a longer break every fourth round. The classic Pomodoro setup works for many people, and it is easy to tune when it does not.

- What size breaker for a cable? Match the breaker to the cable (IEC 60364): The breaker protects the cable, not the other way round. Pick a breaker at or above the load but never above what the cable can carry.

- How much can I spend per day? Work out a daily budget in 5 steps: A daily budget is what is left after bills and savings, divided by the days until payday. One number you can act on at the checkout, recalculated each morning.

- Progesterone timing for dog breeding: when to mate after the LH surge: Progesterone rises from under 1 ng/mL to about 5 ng/mL at ovulation. Test every one to two days and you can pin down the fertile days instead of guessing from the calendar.

- Why didn't my eggs hatch? Causes by candling stage and fixes: Work out why your eggs did not hatch by looking at when they failed: clear at day 7, dead at day 10, or fully formed but dead in the shell. Each stage points to different fixes.

- Best before vs use by: what food date labels really mean: Use by is a safety date and best before is a quality date. Knowing which is which, and when a product is opened, saves food and money without taking risks.

- How to time a whole dinner with several timers at once: A roast, two sides and a sauce all finish at different times. Working backwards from serving time and giving each step its own timer keeps everything hot together.

- What to track in an endometriosis symptom diary (and what to bring to your appointment): Appointments are short and memory is unreliable when you are in pain. A good symptom diary fixes both, if it records the right things.

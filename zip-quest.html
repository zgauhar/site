Zip Quest: number path puzzle

One line. Every number. Every cell. Start on 1, draw a single line through 2, 3, 4… in order and fill the whole grid. A new daily puzzle, endless levels, clever twists and 50 themes to play in.

Free on Google Play. iPhone and iPad version coming soon.

How Zip Quest plays

One line through every number

Drag from 1 to the highest number, passing the numbers in order and covering every cell. The line can't cross itself or go through a wall.

A daily puzzle for everyone

Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. Solve it to keep your streak going and share your result with friends.

Endless levels, 3 stars each

Pick 5×5 warm-up, 6×6 classic, 7×7 tricky or 8×8 expert. "6×6 · Level 37" is the same puzzle on every phone. Earn stars for solving, solving without hints and beating par time.

Dots and portals

Dots tell you where the line must go straight or turn; portals force a jump across the grid. Each twist is explained the first time you meet it, and the clock waits.

50 themes with their own music

A wizard school, a pirate map, a sakura garden, a haunted house, a synthwave grid, a sushi bar, a space mission and 43 more, each with its own line, character, board and music.

Hints and undo

Drag back over the line to undo part of it, or tap Hint when you're stuck. A hint costs the no-hints star on that level.

Questions

How do you play Zip Quest?

Start on the cell marked 1 and drag one line through 2, 3, 4 and every other number in order until the line has filled every cell of the grid. The line can't cross itself or go through a wall. Drag back over the line to undo part of it, or tap Hint when you're stuck.

Is there a new puzzle every day?

Yes. Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. Solve it to keep your streak going and share your result with friends.

What are dots and portals?

They are twists that appear in some levels. A dot tells you whether the line must go straight through that cell or turn there. A portal makes the line jump across the grid to its twin. Each twist is explained the first time you meet it, and the clock waits.

Is Zip Quest free?

Yes. The game is free to play with occasional ads between puzzles and an optional rewarded video for extra stars, on Android and on iPhone. Three themes are free, there is a free theme of the day, and you earn more by collecting stars. Zip Quest Pro unlocks all 50 themes, every theme added later and unlimited hints, and removes the ads. Pro is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase; prices are shown in the app in your currency.

Where can I get Zip Quest?

Zip Quest is free on Google Play for Android phones and tablets. The iPhone and iPad version is coming soon to the App Store.

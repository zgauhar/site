eBay fee calculator for private sellers

Short answer: private sellers registered in the UK pay no final value fee on eBay.co.uk. The buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee of £0.10 + 7% of the price up to £20 + 4% from £20 to £300 + 2% from £300 to £4,000. On a £50.00 item that is £2.70. France and Italy use the same bands in euros since 1 September 2026; Germany is free on both sides for domestic sales; eBay.es still charges private sellers.

How the Buyer Protection fee works

The fee is charged to the buyer on items bought from private sellers, on the item price only (not postage). The fixed part is charged once per item, the percentage is worked out in bands, and nothing is charged on the part of the price above 4,000. Vehicles, classified ads and some other categories are excluded. Selling as a business, selling abroad or using paid extras (promoted listings, extra listings above the free allowance) costs extra and is not included here.

Questions

Do private sellers pay eBay fees in the UK?

Not on most sales: UK-registered private sellers pay no final value fee and no regulatory operating fee. The buyer pays the Buyer Protection fee instead. Extra listings above 300 a month cost 35p each.

How is the eBay Buyer Protection fee calculated?

£0.10 (or €0.10) per item plus 7% of the price up to 20, 4% of the part from 20 to 300 and 2% of the part from 300 to 4,000. Postage is not included.

Is eBay free for private sellers in France and Italy?

Yes, since 1 September 2026 private sellers registered in France or Italy pay no selling fee; buyers pay the Buyer Protection fee.

What does eBay.es charge private sellers?

11.5% of the total sale including postage up to €2,000 and 2% above, plus a 0.42% regulatory fee and €0.35 per order (€0.05 if the order is under €10).

Cable Calc: cable size and voltage drop calculator

Enter the load, the length and how the cable is installed. Cable Calc picks the breaker, applies the derating factors, checks the voltage drop and shows the cable cross-section you need in mm², to IEC 60364.

iPhone version coming soon.

What Cable Calc does

Cable size to IEC 60364-5-52

Current-carrying capacity from Annex B for copper cables from 1.5 to 120 mm², PVC (70 °C) or XLPE/EPR (90 °C), installation methods A1, A2, B1, B2, C and E.

Derating built in

Ambient temperature and grouping correction factors are applied for you, and the breaker is chosen so that Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz.

Voltage drop check

The Annex G method with reactance and power factor: drop in volts and %, voltage at the load, pass or fail against your limit, and the minimum size that meets it.

Ohm's law and power

Enter any two of voltage, current, resistance and power to get the other two. Power to current for single-phase, three-phase and DC.

Every step shown

Intermediate values (Ib, In, Iz, correction factors) are on screen, so you can check the working before you sign off.

Made for site work

Works offline, remembers your last inputs and runs in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Turkish and Dutch.

Questions

Which standard does Cable Calc use?

IEC 60364-5-52: Annex B for current-carrying capacity and the Annex G method for voltage drop. It suits countries that use metric cable sizes, including most of Europe, the Middle East, Africa and India. It is not built for the US NEC (AWG).

Can I rely on the result without checking?

No. Cable Calc gives guidance based on IEC 60364. Always verify against the standard, the cable manufacturer's data and your national wiring regulations. The installer remains responsible for the design.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All tables and calculations are in the app, so it works offline on site.

Is aluminium cable supported?

The capacity tables cover copper conductors from 1.5 to 120 mm².

What normal weight gain looks like

  • A small dip in the first 24–48 hours is normal, while the puppy learns to feed.
  • After that, the weight should go up every day.
  • Most puppies double their birth weight by about day 10. Doubling in 10 days works out at roughly 7% a day, compounded.

How to weigh newborn puppies

  • Use a digital kitchen scale that shows single grams; ounces are too coarse for tiny puppies.
  • Weigh every day for the first two weeks, at the same times (for example morning and evening), and write it down.
  • Use a collar colour or mark to tell the puppies apart, so each weight goes with the right puppy.
  • Compare day to day, not feed to feed: a puppy that has just fed weighs more than one about to feed.

When to call the vet

A puppy that loses weight after the first two days, stops gaining, hasn't doubled its birth weight by day 10, seems weak, cries constantly or isn't feeding needs a vet check. Newborns can go downhill fast, so don't wait.

Frequently asked questions

How much weight should a newborn puppy gain per day?

Enough to double the birth weight in about 10 days, which is roughly 7% of its weight a day on average. A small loss in the first 24–48 hours is normal; after that the weight should go up every day.

My puppy lost a few grams on day 1. Should I worry?

A small dip in the first one to two days is common. A loss after day 2, or a puppy that keeps losing, needs a closer look by your vet.

How often should I weigh newborn puppies?

Daily for the first two weeks, at the same times each day, then every few days until weaning. The Sire & Dam app lets you weigh the whole litter in one go.

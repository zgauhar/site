Days Until: expiration date tracker

Point your camera at a label to scan the date, and Days Until counts down the days and reminds you before groceries, medicine, cosmetics, passports, licences or subscriptions expire.

What Days Until does

Scan the date

On-device text recognition reads best-before and use-by dates from the label. Add your own keywords (for example "mindestens haltbar") so it works with products in any language.

Clear countdowns

Every item shows the days left, sorted by what expires first. Color-coded cards show what's safe, expiring soon or already expired.

Reminders in time

Get a reminder 30 days, 1 week and 1 day before each item expires, so there's time to use it up or renew it.

Food, medicine, cosmetics

Cut food waste on groceries, leftovers and baby food. Keep skincare, sunscreen and medicine within their safe dates.

Documents and renewals

Never miss renewing a passport, ID card, driver's licence, visa, insurance, warranty or certificate.

Used it or tossed it

Mark items as used or thrown away when they leave the fridge, so your list stays honest. If a label only shows month and year, the last day of that month is used.

Questions

Does the scanner read barcodes?

No. It reads the printed date text (best before, use by, expiry) from the label, on your device.

What can I track besides food?

Anything with a date: medicine, cosmetics, passports, ID cards, driver's licences, visas, insurance, warranties, certifications and subscriptions.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Labels are processed on your phone and items are stored only on your device. No account, no ads.

What happens with a date like "OCT 2026"?

If a label only shows month and year, Days Until uses the last day of that month.

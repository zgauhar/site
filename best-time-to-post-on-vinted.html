Best time to post on Vinted

Short answer: Vinted does not publish data on the best time to list. The advice sellers share most often is to post in the evening (roughly 6 pm to 10 pm) and on Sunday, when many people browse from home, and to list seasonal items 4 to 6 weeks before the season starts. Treat these as starting points and check them against your own sales.

Time of day and day of the week

New listings show up near the top of searches and feeds when they are fresh, so it makes sense to publish when buyers are browsing. Sellers in Vinted communities most often name:

  • Weekday evenings, 6 pm to 10 pm: after work and school, on the sofa.
  • Sunday, from late morning to the evening.
  • Lunch breaks (12 to 2 pm) as a smaller second window.
  • Around payday (the end and start of the month), when people have more to spend.

None of these is an official Vinted figure. Your buyers may differ by category and country.

How to find your own best time

  1. List in a batch shortly before your chosen window instead of one item at a time during the day.
  2. Write a complete title and description (brand, size, colour, condition, measurements). A listing that answers questions sells faster at any hour. Our Vinted description templates help.
  3. Keep a note of the day and time each item sold for a month or two, then compare. Relisto keeps the date of every sale and shows which months and platforms sell best.
  4. Check your price against the buyer’s total with the Vinted fees calculator.

Questions

What is the best day to post on Vinted?

Sellers most often recommend Sunday, followed by weekday evenings. Vinted does not publish official figures, so test it with your own items.

What time should I post on Vinted?

The evening, roughly 6 pm to 10 pm, is the window sellers mention most. Lunchtime is a second, smaller window.

When should I list winter coats on Vinted?

From September, around 4 to 6 weeks before the cold weather, so they are visible when buyers start looking.

Does Vinted say when to post?

No. Vinted does not publish a best time to list. The advice on this page is what sellers commonly share.

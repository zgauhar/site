The heater manual comes first

Sauna heater manuals list the supply cable and fuse for each model, and some ask for a bigger fuse than the smallest standard rating, for example 16 A for a 9 kW heater. If yours does, enter the fuse rating as the load in amps: the cable is then sized to carry the fuse. With 16 A, 15 m in an insulated wall needs 2.5 mm²; clipped direct 1.5 mm².

Inside the hot room

This calculator sizes the supply cable up to the sauna, at normal room temperature. The cable from the junction box to the heater runs inside the hot room and must be the heat-resistant type the heater manual specifies. Saunas are a special location in IEC 60364-7-703, with extra rules for where equipment may go, and many countries add their own.

Questions

What size cable for a 9 kW sauna heater?

A 9 kW heater on 400 V three-phase draws about 13.0 A per phase. Over 15 m that is 1.5 mm² clipped direct and 1.5 mm² in an insulated wall, on a 13 A breaker. If the manual asks for a 16 A fuse, size for 16 A instead.

How do I work out the current of a sauna heater?

For a three-phase heater, divide the power in watts by √3 × 400 V. A 9 kW heater: 9000 / (1.732 × 400) ≈ 13 A per phase. For single-phase, divide by 230 V.

Can a sauna heater run on single-phase?

Some small heaters can, but most heaters above about 6 kW are designed for three-phase. The heater manual lists the supplies it accepts and the cable for each.

Can I rely on the result without checking?

No. It is guidance based on IEC 60364. The heater manufacturer's instructions and your national wiring rules come first. The installer remains responsible for the design.

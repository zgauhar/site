Shadow Match: the 3D shadow puzzle

Rotate a 3D shape until its shadow lines up perfectly with the target silhouette. Simple to learn, good for spatial reasoning and deeply satisfying when the shape clicks into place.

What Shadow Match does

Turn the shape, match the shadow

Swipe and the shape turns the way you drag; twist with two fingers to roll it. A live meter shows how aligned you are. Reach 95% to solve.

Daily Shadow

A new puzzle every day, the same for every player. Solve it to grow your streak and earn bonus stars.

Three-star solves

Each solve is rated by how few moves it took. Can you find the angle in four moves or fewer?

Endless levels

Six handcrafted shapes (cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, wedge, gem, T-piece), then an endless stream of generated crystals, prisms and glyphs.

Slider mode

Prefer precision? Switch to sliders and fine-tune each axis by the degree.

No timers, no pressure

Solve at your own pace, with satisfying haptics and a sparkle burst on every match. Works offline.

Questions

Is there a timer?

No. There are no clocks and no pressure; solve each puzzle at your own pace.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Shadow Match works without an internet connection.

What if I get stuck?

Use a hint: it shows which axis to fix and its target angle. You get 3 free hints a day.

Is it a subscription?

No. The game is free to play, and Premium is a one-time purchase.
